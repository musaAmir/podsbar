## Intent

A modern SaaS-style release video for PodsBar, a tiny macOS menu bar app that shows
AirPods battery as two capsules that fill up. For the app's page and social posts
(X, YouTube). Inspired by Zelios-agency SaaS ads (LangEase): fast kinetic type,
UI flying in with depth, punchy cuts. Problem → glance: "How much battery is left?"
→ the clicks it takes today → PodsBar, one glance. Then the name.
